I'm Sangryul Kim, a `Machine Learning Engineer` at [NAVER](https://www.navercorp.com/en), where most of my work is `Agentic AI` for the feeds in the NAVER app. On one side that means LLM agents that draft a feed for a user while a separate judge model critiques the draft; those are still prototypes. On the other side are LLM/VLM pipelines that read articles along with their images and video and score their quality, and those already run in production: the scores feed straight into ranking, so a bad judgment shows up in what people actually see.

I also build `Retrieval` and `Ranking` for the same feeds and test changes through A/B experiments, some of which don't pan out. A surprising share of the job is translation; content-policy and product teams describe what a bad article looks like, and I turn that into rubrics a model can apply the same way every hour.

Before NAVER, I finished an M.S. in AI at [KAIST](https://gsai.kaist.ac.kr/) (Feb. 2025) with Prof. James Thorne in the [XFACT](https://xfact.net/) lab, working on retrieval and RAG for question answering. My COLING 2025 paper trains a reward model to filter out the context a QA system doesn't need. Before that, I interned as an ML engineer at [NAVER WEBTOON](https://www.webtoons.com/en/).

I studied Computer Science and Engineering at Chung-Ang University with a minor in Applied Statistics, and spent part of that time as a software engineer on the [IMQA](https://www.imqa.io/en/) team at [ONYCOM](http://www.onycom.com/en/main.do) building web apps. That's where I got used to shipping things people use, and it's still the part of the job I care about most.
